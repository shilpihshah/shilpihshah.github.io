      About

      I'm Shilpi Shah, an MBA candidate at The Wharton School focused on early-stage venture investing in AI-native companies at Series A and B.

      Before Wharton, I spent four years in M&A advisory at PwC in San Francisco, working on technology transactions: building models, running diligence, and pressure-testing the stories companies tell about themselves. That background shapes how I look at startups. I start from the workflow, the unit economics, and the likely exit, then work back to the pitch.

      I'm most interested in AI with real use cases, especially where software is starting to do the work and not just assist with it.
